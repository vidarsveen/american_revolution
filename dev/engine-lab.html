Does rebuildTo(t) produce the same picture as playing forward to t?
Rule 1 says the picture is a function of time, not a history of events. Everything else on this page is a corollary of that one question.

Does a chart drawn by seeking match one drawn by playing, axis for axis — and does a zero axis still draw?
A chart is the first artifact whose whole content comes out of the pack's entry pools rather than out of the cue, so "same numbers, same picture, however you arrived" is a claim about the resolution as well as about the drawing. The second half is content, not code: every dry wine's sweetness is 0.00, and a bar of width zero and a bar that was never drawn look identical.
